Wenlong Li

Ph.D. Student · University at Buffalo

Department of Civil, Structural and Environmental Engineering

I study how mathematical models can help build more efficient, reliable, and sustainable public transportation systems. I am a Ph.D. student in Transportation Engineering at the University at Buffalo, advised by Dr. Ziqi Song. My research focuses on transportation electrification, system optimization, and network modeling.
